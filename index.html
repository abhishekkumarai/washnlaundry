1. Scope of Service

WashNLaundry provides doorstep pickup, fabric inspection, washing, dry cleaning, steam pressing, and return delivery. All orders are processed in accordance with customer garment care instructions.

2. Intake & Inspection

All garments are inspected upon arrival. Any pre-existing tears, stains, or delicate trims will be documented and communicated before cleaning commences.

3. Payment & Pricing

Zero advance payment required to schedule. Total bill is verified after physical intake count. Payment is collected upon delivery via UPI, cash, or card.

4. Cancellation Policy

You can reschedule or cancel your pickup slot without any penalty up to 1 hour prior to the scheduled window.
