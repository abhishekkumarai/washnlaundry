# WashNLaundry — Shop Policies & FAQ

## Delivery
Home pickup and delivery are available, subject to the shop's configured
service area and time slots. Each order carries a delivery charge line on
the receipt, shown separately from the garment subtotal.

## Express Service
Express orders are charged at the shop's express multiplier (typically
1.5x the standard price) for faster turnaround, usually same-day or
next-day depending on load.

## Order Statuses
An order moves through: Pending → Processing → Ironing → Ready → Out for
Delivery → Delivered. An order can also be Cancelled at any stage before
Delivered.

## Payment
Orders can be Paid, Partial, or Unpaid. Customers can pay in advance,
partially, or on delivery (cash, UPI, or card depending on the shop's
setup). "Dues" refers to the outstanding balance on Partial/Unpaid orders.

## Garment Pricing
Pricing varies by garment and service type (Dry Clean, Wash & Iron,
Wash & Fold, Steam Press, Iron-only). Use the live rate card tool to look
up an exact price for a specific item rather than guessing — prices can
change and vary per shop.

## Damages & Complaints
If a garment is damaged or lost, contact the shop directly — this varies
per shop's own policy and is typically handled case by case, often with a
partial refund or store credit.

## Order Tracking
Customers can track an order's status using their order number or the
tracking link sent at drop-off.
